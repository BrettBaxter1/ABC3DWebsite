Welcome to ABC3D

ABC3D is 3D polygonal modeling software that is designed to be straightforward and intuitive for beginners to use. With other modeling software often being very complex and confusing, ABC3D is meant to offer a less frustrating, less overwhelming, and more enjoyable experience that is ideal for learning and artistic experimentation. In addition to having intuitive interfaces and interactions, ABC3D will provide users with detailed documentation of features and pre-loaded tutorials we create to help users get started. It will also include a tutorial system where users can import tutorials that can be easily viewed and navigated within the application.
